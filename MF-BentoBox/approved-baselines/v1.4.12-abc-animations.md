# MF BentoBoxV1 — Approved A/B/C intro animations (v1.4.12)

**Owner-approved baseline:** 2026-11-20. The owner explicitly approved all three bank-change intros and the global startup sequence after testing the `v1.4.12` experimental ZIP. Treat these animation behaviors as the accepted versions for Banks A, B and C until the owner requests changes.

## Approved global boot — 5.0 seconds

| Time | Segment | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| 0.000–1.625 s | Bank A | RGB columns left → right, then white LED rings right → left. Top-to-bottom: purple, blue, yellow, red. |
| 1.625–3.3125 s | Bank B | RGB rows bottom → top in VU colors (green, yellow, orange, red), then white rings left → right. |
| 3.3125–4.625 s | Bank C | Serpentine 16-step sequencer chase with purple/cyan/yellow RGB and white-ring moving playhead. |
| 4.625–5.000 s | Ready | Full-grid colored ready state, white LED ring pulse. |

The runtime target is five seconds. Earlier builds used four seconds and reported possible early interruption. Owner approval applies to the user-tested v1.4.12 animation result; no generalized guarantee is made about different host startup conditions.

## Approved physical bank-change intros — 1.0 second each

- **Bank A / Bank 1:** Color columns appear left → right; then separate white LED ring sweep right → left. RGB palette by row, top → bottom: purple, blue, yellow, red.
- **Bank B / Bank 2:** Color rows appear bottom → top in VU palette green → yellow → orange → red; then separate white LED ring sweep by columns left → right.
- **Bank C / Bank 3:** Purple, cyan and yellow 16-step serpentine sequencer chase with moving white LED-ring playhead and final pulse.
- **Bank D:** Reserved. Do not emit animation hardware MIDI for Bank D.

RGB color output and white ring output are independent MIDI channels/effects. Keep the normal bank functions and MIDI mappings unchanged.
